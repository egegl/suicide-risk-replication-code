# Annotation guidelines

Label each post as the original dataset annotators would have, from the text alone. The tool shows the official
definition next to every risk level and factor.

- **Label what the post says, for the writer.** Don't infer what the author (or the model that wrote it) meant
  to express. Other people's suicidality is a factor, not the writer's risk level.
- **Risk level:** the highest level the text explicitly supports.
- **Self-harm:** Attempt means the writer's own suicide attempt. A self-harm act, past or
  ongoing, together with a suicidal expression is Behavior, and past self-harm is also the prior self-harm factor.
  Self-harm that was only considered ("nearly cut") is not an act.
- **Evidence:** for Ideation and above, highlight the short phrase or phrases that justify the risk level. The
  original annotators usually marked one or two phrases of a few words. Indicator posts get none.
- **Factors:** tick every factor the post expresses; none is a valid answer.
- **Unclear cases:** when the definitions leave room, make your best call, mark it unclear, and add a one-line note.
- **Naturalness** (1 to 5): could a person plausibly have written this?
- **Stay independent:** don't discuss cases with the other annotators until all of you have finished.
